        How it works

        Six steps, every six hours. No lock-ups, no claiming, no staking.

        1. STEP 1

          Fees land in the vault

          Creator fees from the token accumulate as SOL in one public wallet. Its balance minus a small fee reserve is the pool.

        2. STEP 2

          A snapshot is taken

          Every 6 hours, at a random offset within ±10 minutes of the mark, so the exact moment cannot be gamed.

        3. STEP 3

          Holders are filtered

          Wallets holding at least 0.1% of supply are eligible. Liquidity pools, the vault and listed exclusions are removed.

        4. STEP 4

          Your picks become demand

          Each holder's balance is spread across their chosen stocks. No saved basket means the default: the top five by market cap, 20% each.

        5. STEP 5

          One swap per stock

          The pool is split by total demand and routed through Jupiter, one SOL-to-stock swap per stock, with a slippage cap. A failed swap keeps its SOL for the next round.

        6. STEP 6

          Stocks land in your wallet

          Tokens are transferred directly to each holder. The project pays the token-account rent. Amounts too small to send are carried into the next round.
